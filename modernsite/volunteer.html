Volunteer

Help make food pickup reliable.

We are building a small driver and coordination network for southern New Hampshire. The first need is simple: reliable people who can help move food when pickup opportunities are available.

Ways to help

Pickup drivers

Help pick up donated food from local retail and community partners.

Delivery support

Move food to food banks, pantries, and hunger relief partners.

Coordination help

Assist with scheduling, route confirmation, outreach, and follow-up.
