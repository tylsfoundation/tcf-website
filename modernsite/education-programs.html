Programs

Educational initiatives connect guidance with opportunity.

TCF is developing program pathways that support mentoring, career conversations, practical skills guidance, and partnerships with people who can help learners see what comes next.

Mentoring

Steady guidance

Mentors can help students, young adults, and career changers think through options, build confidence, and plan next steps.

Career pathways

Real-world direction

Programs can introduce people to business, STEM, skilled trades, and other practical routes into meaningful work.

Partnerships

Community connections

TCF can work with schools, employers, volunteers, and local leaders to connect learners with resources and opportunity.
