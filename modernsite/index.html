The missing link

Food can be available and still not move.

Retailers and community partners often have food ready to donate. Food banks and pantries are ready to put it to use. The fragile part is the local pickup: vehicle space, timing, fuel, loading, and reliable drivers.

Donation programs already work.

Capacity is limited

One missed pickup matters.

If a driver is unavailable or a load is too large for one vehicle, donated food may not reach the organizations waiting for it.

Reliable local transportation.
