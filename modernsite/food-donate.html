Make A Difference Today

Every donation makes a direct impact in the lives of individuals and families facing food insecurity. Your generosity helps us provide nutritious meals and ensure reliable transportation so food reaches those who need it most. Together we can strengthen our community and bring hope to our neighbors.

Your monetary donations help provide:

  • Fresh and nutritious food for individuals and families in need
  • Transportation costs for food pickup and delivery
  • Emergency food assistance for families facing unexpected hardships
  • Supplies needed to safely store, package, and distribute food

Giving Thoughts:

  • $25 - Feeds A FamilyProviding fresh food and essential groceries for a family in need.
  • $50 - Delivers HopeHelps cover fuel and transportation costs to pick up and deliver food to community members.
  • $100 - Sustains Our MissionSupports food purchases, vehicle maintenance, and emergency assistance to ensure we can continue serving those who rely on us.
  • $500 - Power Our GrowthAllows us to partner with other communities for greater support of families and children.
