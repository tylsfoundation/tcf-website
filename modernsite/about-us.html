About Us

Our Mission

The Tyls Charitable Foundation Inc. has been established to provide help to those with food insecurities. By raising money, we will be making food and monetary donations to local food banks and individuals/families to help ease the need. Additionally, we will be working to support individuals interested in pursuing careers in business and STEM fields.

Our Founder, Charles J. (Chuck) Tyls, founded our organization to address two critical needs: food insecurity and access to education. He brings decades of professional experience advising mid-sized through Fortune 500 companies on the design and implementation of financial systems. As an entrepreneur who has owned and operated multiple small businesses, he understands both the challenges organizations face and the importance of strong community support.

Chuck supporting the cause, beyond his professional career, the founder has dedicated countless hours to nonprofit work through fundraising efforts, charitable donations, and hands-on volunteer service. Time spent preparing and serving meals at a local homeless shelter provided firsthand insight into the growing food needs within the community and reinforced the importance of compassionate action. Combined with his extensive experience developing and delivering training programs, this inspired a broader mission: to not only help provide food assistance to those in need, but also to support students pursuing educational opportunities through guidance, mentorship, and financial assistance. Our organization is committed to creating lasting impact by helping individuals and families build a stronger future.
